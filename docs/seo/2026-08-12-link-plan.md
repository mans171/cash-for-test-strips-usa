# Link Acquisition Plan — cash4teststripsusa.com
**Date:** 2026-08-12
**Target:** 50–150 referring domains over 12 months
**Strongest asset:** Proprietary payout tier data (`lib/tier-pricing.ts`) + national buyer directory

---

## Why Links Matter Here Specifically

Domain is brand new (GSC verified 2026-08-10, 0 clicks). For a transactional directory competing on geo-modified keywords like "sell test strips near me," links serve two purposes: (1) raw authority to compete against established buyers' own sites, and (2) crawl budget signals telling Google the city pages are worth indexing. At 5 indexed pages out of 137 submitted, crawl budget is the immediate problem — links accelerate it.

---

## Tactic A: Payout Data PR (highest leverage, 10–20 domains)

**The angle:** No public, structured source currently tracks what buyers pay for diabetic test strips by brand or tier. The site has proprietary tier data, and as the buyer directory grows it will have real transaction-level pricing signals. Publish a **quarterly Test Strip Price Index**: which brands pay most, which are trending up/down, regional variation by state.

**Content to build:** A dedicated `/price-index` or `/research/test-strip-payout-report` page. Updated quarterly. Include: top/mid/lower tier rankings by brand (already in `tier-pricing.ts`), which brands local vs. mail-in buyers prefer, regional payout variation as buyer data grows.

**Target publications:**
- DiabetesMine (diabetesmine.com) — largest diabetes news/advocacy site; they regularly cover cost and financial angles
- diatribe.org — nonprofit diabetes media; strong domain authority; covers affordability
- Diabetes Daily (diabetesdaily.com) — community + editorial; covers "make money" and financial angles
- Healthline (healthline.com) — high-DA health publisher; they cover "how to make money on medical supplies" fringe topics
- The Penny Hoarder (thepennyhoarder.com) — personal finance; covers side income from medical supplies

**Pitch angle:** "We run the largest directory of test strip buyers in the US. Here's what buyers are actually paying — and which brands are worth more than sellers realize."

**Effort:** High (requires building the data product and a one-pager pitch). **Timeline:** 8–12 weeks to first placement. **Expected yield:** 5–10 editorial links, 2–4 major ones.

## Tactic B: Buyer Backlinks (fast, 10–20 domains)

Every listed buyer company gets a free directory listing. Ask each one to link back to their listing page (`/company/[slug]`) or to the directory. This is easy because there's a direct value exchange: they got traffic, you get a link.

**Outreach:** Simple email to each buyer. "We built you a free listing page at [URL]. If you have a website, a link back helps both of us show up higher in searches. Here's the code: `<a href="...">Find us on Cash For Test Strips USA</a>`."

**Target:** 29 current buyers; realistically 10–15 have websites. Expect 6–10 links.

**Effort:** Low. **Timeline:** 1–2 weeks. **Expected yield:** 8–12 domains.

---

## Tactic C: Diabetes Community / Forum Mentions (ongoing, 5–15 domains)

**Targets:**
- TuDiabetes / Diabetes Connect community forums
- Reddit r/diabetes, r/diabetestype2, r/mildlyinteresting — nofollow but real traffic that converts to branded searches which improve GSC signals
- Facebook groups for diabetics (traffic, not links, but feeds branded search volume)
- DiabetesSisters.org (community resource; links to practical tools)
- JDRF local chapter resource pages

**Angle:** Be genuinely useful, not promotional. Answer "what do I do with extra test strips" questions with a link to the legality page or directory. The legality page (`/is-it-legal-to-sell-diabetic-test-strips`) is the strongest bait here — it answers a real question with no sales pressure.

**Effort:** Low-Medium (ongoing community participation). **Timeline:** Immediate start, ongoing. **Expected yield:** 3–8 dofollow domains, significant referral traffic.

---

## Tactic D: Estate Sale / Caregiver Resource Sites (5–10 domains)

The biggest untapped seller segment is estate liquidators and caregivers cleaning out a deceased relative's supplies. These people search "what to do with diabetic supplies after death" and similar queries.

**Targets:**
- NASMM.org (National Assoc. of Senior Move Managers) — resource directory
- Estate sale company blogs (search: "estate sale tips site:estatesales.net")
- Senior living resource sites (aplaceformom.com, seniorliving.org)
- Caregiver action groups (caregiveraction.org, caregiver.org)

**Pitch:** "When a loved one passes, unused diabetic supplies can be sold for cash instead of thrown away. Here's how." Offer to write a guest resource or contribute a listing.

**Effort:** Medium. **Timeline:** 4–8 weeks per placement. **Expected yield:** 5–10 domains.

---

## Tactic E: Local Media Placements (5–10 domains)

City-level local news sites have high domain authority and are underutilized by this niche. Once city pages are live (Wave 1), pitch a local angle.

**Pitch template:** "Albany-area residents are making $50–$300 selling unused diabetes supplies — here's where to go." Target local TV station websites, alt-weeklies, and city news blogs in each T1 market.

**Best targets:** Local ABC/NBC/CBS affiliates (e.g., WNYT Albany, WBTV Charlotte) — their websites often accept story pitches and link to local resources.

**Effort:** Medium-High (requires good city pages to be live first). **Timeline:** Weeks 6–16 (after Wave 1 pages index). **Expected yield:** 5–10 domains with good DA.

---

## Tactic F: Medical Equipment / Healthcare Directory Listings (5–10 domains)

Low-effort foundational links. Directory submissions to:
- Healthgrades (healthgrades.com)
- Medical supply directories (medicalsupplydirectory.com, etc.)
- Better Business Bureau listing
- Chamber of commerce sites in markets where buyers operate

**Effort:** Low. **Timeline:** 1–3 weeks. **Expected yield:** 5–8 domains, mostly lower DA but clean signals for a new site.

## Anchor Text Strategy

Do not over-optimize anchors. Because this is a new domain, unnatural anchor distribution will be flagged.

| Anchor type | Target share | Examples |
|-------------|-------------|----------|
| Brand name | 40% | "Cash For Test Strips USA," "cash4teststripsusa.com" |
| Naked URL | 20% | "https://cash4teststripsusa.com" |
| Natural phrase | 30% | "this directory," "test strip buyers near you," "where to sell test strips" |
| Exact-match keyword | 10% max | "sell diabetic test strips" — use sparingly |
